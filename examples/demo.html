Moving cards

Drag with a mouse or with touch. A dashed placeholder shows where the card will land, and the column under the pointer is highlighted. Links and buttons on a card don't start a drag.

The info button and links

The paragraphs of an article become the card's description, shown with the “ⓘ Info” button. The first link becomes the card's link. Links to a place on the same page — like the ones on these cards — scroll there; any other link opens in a new tab. With a card focused, Enter follows its link.

Saved progress

Only card IDs, their column and their order are stored, in your browser's localStorage under the board's id (kanban-state:board-en here). Give every board a unique, stable id. The content is always read fresh from the page. The “Reset” button under the board — or a call to the API — puts every card back into the first column.

EdoKanban.reset(document.getElementById('board-en'));

The markup

A board is a section with data-type="kanban". Each direct article[data-card] child is a task: a unique data-card ID, an h3 title, optional paragraphs and an optional link. New cards start in the first column; data-columns names the columns.

<section id="my-board" class="kanban kanban-pending" data-type="kanban"
         data-columns='["To do", "Doing", "Done"]'>
  <article data-card="task-1">
    <h3>Write the report</h3>
    <p>Outline first, then the draft.</p>
    <a href="#report">Notes ↓</a>
  </article>
</section>
